Our Story
ABOUT
REVOLUTION
MOTOR WORKS

Revolution Motor Works was built on a simple idea: every customer deserves honest, high-quality auto repair at a fair price. We're a family-owned shop located in Finksburg, Maryland, proud to serve the Pikesville area and surrounding communities.

Over the past 12+ years, we've earned a reputation for doing the work right — the first time. Our team brings deep expertise across all vehicle makes and models, from everyday commuters to performance vehicles. We invest in proper tools and training so you get dealer-level knowledge without the dealer-level markup.

With nearly 800 five-star Google reviews, our track record speaks for itself. But we never take that trust for granted — every car that comes through our door gets our full attention and care.

Transparency. We show you what's wrong and explain your options before we do anything.
Quality. We use proper parts and back our work. No shortcuts.
Community. Finksburg is our home — we take pride in taking care of our neighbors.
